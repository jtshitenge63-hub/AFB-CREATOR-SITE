AfriBook for Creators

About

AfriBook for Creators, abbreviated AFB Creator, is a partner product of the AfriBook social network, designed by researcher Tshitenge Jean Claude.

It was designed to help all content creators, whether or not they use AfriBook, save on their mobile data plans and on their phone storage.

We noticed that after content is created and edited on some platforms, videos become heavier, use a lot of data when uploaded to social networks, and take up a lot of storage space on the phone.

AFB Creator solves this problem with its compression feature, which greatly reduces the size of videos while preserving their visual quality. This lets you upload lighter videos to social networks while keeping excellent visual quality.

We also offer other features, such as converting videos to audio files, batch compression, image compression, video editing and quality filter options, and more.

All of this is built for content creators around the world.
